SeerrNG for Android Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

SeerrNG for Android (package org.snapetech.seerrng) is maintained by Snapetech. It connects to SeerrNG servers that you choose and configure. The app does not provide a server or create a Snapetech account for you.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the server names and addresses you save in its private app storage. When you sign in, Android WebView stores the site data and session cookies needed to keep you signed in to that server. The app may use the selected server's session cookie when downloading a file from that same server. Capped-use builds also store the number of successful server sessions used for the free preview. This count is kept on your device, is used only to decide whether another app session can start, and is not sent to Snapetech or to the SeerrNG server. You can remove a saved server or clear the app's storage in Android settings; clearing storage resets the local preview count, and uninstalling the app removes its app storage.

Your sign-in and normal app activity are sent directly to the server address you selected. The server operator controls the server's data collection, retention, and access. Review that operator's privacy information as well. If you choose an HTTP address, the connection is not encrypted in transit; the app warns before allowing it.

Advertising and consent

Some release builds may show a Google AdMob banner while you browse a saved server. The app requests non-personalized ads. This limits personalization; it does not turn off all advertising-related data processing. When enabled, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the device's IP address, app interactions (such as launches and taps), diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID) for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google states that this SDK data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and how Google uses data when you use partner apps.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) displays applicable privacy messages and manages the consent choices used by the ad SDK. Google may store or process consent signals to apply those choices. When the app shows a Privacy and data choices control, use it to review or change your choices. Android also provides controls for the advertising ID in system settings. The app does not include an analytics or crash-reporting service operated by Snapetech.

Google Play purchases

If the Google Play version offers the optional lifetime unlock, Google Play processes the checkout and transaction. The app receives purchase status from Google Play and stores an on-device entitlement so the purchase can remove ads in an ad-supported build or unlock unlimited sessions in a capped-use build. The app does not send the purchase token to a Snapetech server. Google handles payment information under its own privacy policy. APKs installed outside Google Play do not provide an alternate payment flow.

Information shared with others

  • Your server address, sign-in activity, and requests go to the SeerrNG server you selected, as described above.
  • When ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and UMP process the data described in the advertising section. Applicable ad technology providers are shown in Google's consent message.
  • Google Play receives information needed to process and manage in-app purchases.

Snapetech does not receive the saved server list, server sign-in credentials, WebView session cookies, browsing activity, or purchase token from this app.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can remove saved servers, sign out through your server, or clear the app's data using Android settings. When the app presents a privacy-choice control, it opens Google's UMP form so you can change or withdraw ad consent where available. You can also manage the Android advertising ID using system settings. To ask a privacy question or request help, contact keith@snape.tech.

Children

SeerrNG for Android is not designed for children under 13, and Snapetech does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or the services it uses change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

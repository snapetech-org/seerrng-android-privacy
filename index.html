SeerrNG Mobile Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

SeerrNG for Android (package org.snapetech.seerrng) and SeerrNG for iOS (bundle identifier org.snapetech.seerrng.ios) are maintained by Snapetech. They connect to SeerrNG servers that you choose and configure. The apps do not provide a server or create a Snapetech account for you.

Information stored on your device

The apps store the server names and addresses you save in private app storage. When you sign in, Android WebView or iOS WebKit stores the site data and session cookies needed to keep you signed in. Each server has a separate browser profile. Removing a saved server also removes its browser profile and cookies. Android may use that server's session cookie when downloading a file from that same server.

Capped-preview builds store the number of successful server sessions used for the free preview. This count stays on your device, is used only to decide whether another app session can start, and is not sent to Snapetech or to the SeerrNG server. Clearing app data or reinstalling resets the local preview count. Uninstalling the app removes its local app storage.

Your sign-in and normal app activity are sent directly to the server address you selected. The server operator controls that server's data collection, retention, and access. Review the operator's privacy information as well. HTTPS is recommended. If you choose an HTTP address, the connection is not encrypted in transit; the app warns before allowing it.

Advertising and consent

Some release builds may show a Google AdMob banner while you browse a saved server. The app requests non-personalized ads. This limits personalization; it does not turn off all advertising-related data processing. When enabled, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the device's IP address, app interactions (such as launches and taps), diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID) for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google states that this SDK data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and how Google uses data when you use partner apps.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) displays applicable privacy messages and manages the consent choices used by the ad SDK. Google may store or process consent signals to apply those choices. When the app shows a Privacy and data choices control, use it to review or change your choices. Android also provides controls for the advertising ID in system settings. The app does not include an analytics or crash-reporting service operated by Snapetech.

Store purchases

If a store version offers the optional lifetime unlock, Google Play or Apple's App Store processes the checkout and transaction. The app checks the store entitlement and uses it to remove ads in an Android ad-supported build or unlock unlimited sessions in a capped-preview build. The app does not send purchase tokens to a Snapetech server. Google and Apple handle payment information under their own privacy policies. Android APKs installed outside Google Play do not provide a separate checkout flow.

Information shared with others

  • Your server address, sign-in activity, and requests go to the SeerrNG server you selected, as described above.
  • When ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and UMP process the data described in the advertising section. Applicable ad technology providers are shown in Google's consent message.
  • Google Play receives information needed to process and manage in-app purchases.
  • Apple's App Store processes any StoreKit purchase and provides the app with the resulting entitlement state.

Snapetech does not receive the saved server list, server sign-in credentials, browser session cookies, browsing activity, local preview counter, or purchase tokens from these apps. The iOS app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. The Android app does not include a Snapetech-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can remove saved servers and their browser profiles, sign out through your server, or clear the app's data in your device settings. When an Android ad-supported build presents a privacy-choice control, it opens Google's UMP form so you can change or withdraw ad consent where available. You can also manage the Android advertising ID in system settings. To ask a privacy question or request help, contact support@snape.tech.

Children

SeerrNG mobile apps are not designed for children under 13, and Snapetech does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through these apps.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or the services it uses change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

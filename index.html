SeerrNG Mobile Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

SeerrNG for Android (package org.snapetech.seerrng) and SeerrNG for iOS (bundle identifier org.snapetech.seerrng.ios) are maintained by Snapetech. They connect to SeerrNG servers that you choose and configure. The apps do not provide a server or create a Snapetech account for you.

Information stored on your device

The apps store the server names and addresses you save in private app storage. When you sign in, Android WebView or iOS WebKit stores the site data and session cookies needed to keep you signed in. Each server has a separate browser profile. Removing a saved server also removes its browser profile and cookies. Android may use that server's session cookie when downloading a file from that same server.

The apps store the number of successful server sessions used for the free preview. This count stays on your device, is used only to decide whether another app session can start, and is not sent to Snapetech or to the SeerrNG server. Clearing app data or reinstalling resets the local preview count. Uninstalling the app removes its local app storage.

Your sign-in and normal app activity are sent directly to the server address you selected. The server operator controls that server's data collection, retention, and access. Review the operator's privacy information as well. HTTPS is recommended. If you choose an HTTP address, the connection is not encrypted in transit; the app warns before allowing it.

Advertising and analytics

The apps do not display advertising and do not include an advertising, analytics, or Snapetech-operated crash-reporting SDK.

Store purchases

If a store version offers the optional lifetime unlock, Google Play or Apple's App Store processes the checkout and transaction. The app checks the store entitlement and uses it to unlock unlimited sessions. The app does not send purchase tokens to a Snapetech server. Google and Apple handle payment information under their own privacy policies. Android APKs installed outside Google Play do not provide a separate checkout flow.

Information shared with others

  • Your server address, sign-in activity, and requests go to the SeerrNG server you selected, as described above.
  • Google Play receives information needed to process and manage in-app purchases.
  • Apple's App Store processes any StoreKit purchase and provides the app with the resulting entitlement state.

Snapetech does not receive the saved server list, server sign-in credentials, browser session cookies, browsing activity, local preview counter, or purchase tokens from these apps.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can remove saved servers and their browser profiles, sign out through your server, or clear the app's data in your device settings. To ask a privacy question or request help, contact support@snape.tech.

Children

SeerrNG mobile apps are not designed for children under 13, and Snapetech does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through these apps.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or the services it uses change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
